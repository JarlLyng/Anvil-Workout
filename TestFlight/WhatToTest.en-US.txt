1.10.1 keeps a workout on screen when the layout changes under it, and is built for iPhone Duo. What to try:

- On iPad: start a workout, then make the window narrow in Split View or Stage Manager, and wide again. The workout should stay on screen with its rest timer running, and the watch should keep following it.
- On iPhone Duo: unfolded, the app fills the inner display with the sidebar and the two-column workout. Fold and unfold during a workout; it should carry on the same way.
- Settings, Send Feedback: an email to support in your mail app, with the app and iOS version filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.
- In a workout, tap Plates on a set with a weight. The calculator should open on that weight the first time, too.

The App Store review request never appears in TestFlight builds, so there is nothing to test there.
