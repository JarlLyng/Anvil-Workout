Frequently Asked Questions

Does Anvil Workout require a subscription?

No. Anvil Workout is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no in-app purchases, and no hidden costs. You pay once and get all features forever, including future updates.

Does Anvil Workout work offline?

Yes. Anvil Workout needs no internet connection to plan, run or review a workout. Your programs, sets and history are stored on your iPhone and are never sent to a server. There is no account and no cloud dependency. The only thing the app sends is a crash report if it crashes, and that contains no workout or Health data.

Does it sync with Apple Health?

Yes, if you allow it. Each completed workout is saved to Apple Health. Anvil then reads back the calories and average heart rate that Health recorded during the workout and shows them in your history. Those numbers come from another source, usually an Apple Watch, so without one they are often empty.

Does the Apple Watch app work on its own?

No. Start the workout on your iPhone and the watch follows it, so you can log and skip sets from your wrist. It needs no internet, but it does need your iPhone nearby.

Does my data sync between iPhone and iPad?

No. Each device keeps its own programs and history, and nothing syncs between them. The Apple Watch is different because it shows the workout running on your iPhone instead of keeping its own.

What happens to my data if I get a new iPhone?

It moves with your device backup. If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your Anvil data is included and comes back when you restore to the new phone. Without a backup it stays on the old one. You can also export your workout history as a CSV file from Settings at any time.

What can I track?

You can create full workout programs with exercises, sets, reps, weight targets, rest timers, and supersets. During workouts, sets are tracked in real time on your iPhone (with Live Activity) or on your wrist using our native Apple Watch companion app, which features haptic rest alerts. After workouts, you can review volume charts, weekly frequency, estimated 1RM progression, muscle group distribution, and automatically detected personal records.

How is Anvil Workout different from other gym apps?

Anvil Workout is built for lifters who want a focused, distraction-free gym experience. Unlike apps that require monthly subscriptions and cloud accounts, Anvil Workout is a one-time purchase that works offline and keeps your workouts on your device. It includes native support for iPad (ideal for gym stands) and an Apple Watch companion that follows the workout on your iPhone so you can log sets from your wrist, alongside Live Activity and Apple Health integration.
