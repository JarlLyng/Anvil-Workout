Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Summary

Iron Workout does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your workout data stays on your device and in your iCloud account (via device backup).

Data storage

All data — workout programs, session history, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is sent to any server.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Iron Workout writes workout sessions to Apple Health and reads calories burned and heart rate data. This data is handled entirely by Apple's HealthKit framework and stays within Apple's Health ecosystem. Iron Workout does not access, export, or transmit Health data beyond what HealthKit provides.

Crash reporting

Iron Workout uses Sentry for anonymous crash reporting and performance monitoring. This helps us find and fix bugs. Sentry may collect:

  • Device model and OS version
  • App version and build number
  • Anonymous crash stack traces

No personal data, workout data, or Health data is included in crash reports.

Analytics

Iron Workout does not use any analytics or tracking services. There are no ads.

Third-party services

The only external service used is Sentry (crash reporting). No data is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

Children's privacy

Iron Workout does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@iamjarl.com.
