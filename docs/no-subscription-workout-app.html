A Workout Tracker You Pay For Once

Anvil Workout is a one-time purchase. There's no subscription, no in-app purchase and no account, and every feature is included on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This page sets out what that gets you, what it doesn't, and the questions worth asking of any tracker you compare it with.

What a subscription can pay for

A subscription isn't a worse model. It pays for different things. Running servers lets a tracker keep your history in an account, sync it between devices, offer a web version, and add social features, coaching or generated programs. A one-time purchase can't easily carry those costs, so Anvil Workout doesn't offer them.

Plans also differ more than the word suggests. Some trackers have a free tier, some sell a lifetime option beside the subscription, and what stays available when you stop paying varies from app to app. Check the plan you're considering on its maker's own pages.

Questions to ask of any tracker

These decide whether your training history stays yours. Here is how Anvil Workout answers each one.

QuestionAnvil Workout
What does it cost?One payment on the App Store, in your currency. No subscription and no in-app purchases.
Is anything behind a further payment?No. Every feature is included, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
Can my family use it?Yes. It supports Family Sharing, so up to six family members can use it.

Can I bring my history in?From Strong or Hevy, as a CSV file. How the import works.

What you give up

Anvil Workout has no server, and that has a cost. Your iPhone and iPad don't sync with each other, there's no web or Android version, and there are no social features, coaching or generated programs. Your history is as safe as your device backup, because there is no copy of it in an account.

Who Anvil Workout is for

If you follow your own program, want to log fast, would rather pay once and like your training history on your own devices, Anvil Workout is built for you. If you want a feed, a leaderboard, a coach, or your history on several devices at once, a tracker built around an account will suit you better.
